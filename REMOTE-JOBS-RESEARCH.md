## TL;DR

For a Philippines-based developer, priority order is:
**Arc.dev → Wellfound → RemoteOK → WeWorkRemotely → Remote100K**

Most platforms are US-company-dominated even when they claim "worldwide." Arc.dev is the exception — it actively recruits and pre-vets international developers, then surfaces you to global employers.

---

## Platform Breakdown

### 1. Arc.dev — Best for PH/Global Devs ⭐
- **Global reach:** 190 countries, actively recruits internationally
- **Vetting:** 4-stage pipeline (screening, live coding, role-specific assessment, soft skills) — once in, employers come to you
- **Salary transparency:** 7 regional tiers published openly
  - Asia avg: varies by role
  - North America avg: ~$82,757
  - Africa avg: ~$53,333
  - Oceania avg: ~$86,004
- **Job types:** Contract and full-time
- **Geographic restrictions:** Minimal — designed for global talent
- **Dev-friendliness:** High. Technical vetting means less noise, better matches.
- **Verdict:** Highest ROI for a PH-based senior dev. Apply here first.

### 2. RemoteOK
- **Global reach:** 8 geographic filters — Worldwide, North America, Latin America, Europe, Africa, Middle East, Asia, Oceania + individual country filters
- **Positioning:** Explicitly "work from anywhere" — not employer-country-restricted
- **Job types:** Software engineering, DevOps, data science, design — skews startup/distributed teams
- **Salary transparency:** Partial — some listings show ranges, not enforced
- **Geographic restrictions:** Many individual listings still US-only even under "worldwide" tag
- **Dev-friendliness:** High. Clean filters, no account required to browse.
- **Verdict:** Good firehose. Use the Asia/Worldwide filter and check each listing for restrictions.

### 3. Wellfound (formerly AngelList Talent)
- **Global reach:** Startup-focused, international companies list here
- **Standout feature:** Single application reaches 130,000+ jobs simultaneously
- **Salary transparency:** 82% salary disclosure rate (2026) — best in class
- **Job types:** Full-time, startup equity common
- **Geographic restrictions:** Varies by listing; many early-stage companies are remote-first globally
- **Dev-friendliness:** High. Equity + salary shown upfront on most roles.
- **Verdict:** Best for startup roles. Set up profile once, get discovered.

### 4. WeWorkRemotely
- **Global reach:** Claims largest remote job board; in practice North America/Europe dominant
- **Job types:** Full-time and contract/part-time filters
- **Notable employers:** Amazon, Google, Basecamp, and similar
- **Salary transparency:** Low — most listings don't show ranges
- **Geographic restrictions:** High — even "Anywhere in the World" listings often restrict to specific timezones or exclude certain countries
- **Dev-friendliness:** Medium. High volume, but lots of filtering needed.
- **Verdict:** Use as supplementary. Don't rely on it as primary source from PH.

### 5. Remote100K
- **Global reach:** Curated only — manually reviewed listings
- **Salary floor:** Every listing is $100K+ with explicit salary range shown upfront
- **Standout feature:** No account required to apply — direct application links
- **Example listing seen:** $266,000–$372,400 for Reddit ML Engineer role
- **Geographic restrictions:** MANY listings are US-only despite appearing on a "remote" board
- **Dev-friendliness:** High quality but low volume.
- **Verdict:** Good for benchmarking salaries and catching high-value outliers. Not a primary source for PH devs due to US restrictions.

## Key Patterns Verified

1. **"Remote" ≠ "Global"** — Even platforms with worldwide filters serve mostly US employers. Always check the individual listing for country restrictions before investing time in an application.

2. **Pre-vetted pools outperform job boards** — Arc.dev and similar platforms (Lemon.io, Flexiple) that vet you once and surface you to employers repeatedly have higher ROI than applying one-by-one on boards.

3. **Salary transparency correlates with global-friendliness** — Platforms that show pay upfront (Arc.dev, Wellfound, Remote100K) tend to be more serious about international candidates. Opaque salary boards tend to have more US-restriction fine print.

4. **Timezone flexibility is the real gate** — Many "worldwide" roles implicitly require US/EU timezone overlap. Cebu (UTC+8) overlaps with Eastern Europe morning and US West Coast afternoon — worth calling out in applications.
